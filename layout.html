Climate · Antalya, Turkey

CLIMATE TALKS COLLAPSE

After two weeks of wrangling and two days of overtime, COP31 ends with no deal at all — the longest climate summit in history, and the first outright breakdown since Copenhagen in 2009.

A PACT WITH NO FOSSIL FUELS IN IT The final “Antalya Pact” draft dropped every mention of coal, oil and gas. The EU, Panama, the Marshall Islands, Norway and the UK refused it; EU negotiators walked out. President Murat Kurum conceded: “There is no agreement on this text.” UN chief António Guterres said he was “deeply disappointed” — “there is no ambiguity in the science.”

Ukraine · War

Drone flattens flats near Kyiv; 14 missing

A Russian drone struck a five-storey apartment block in Dymer, north of Kyiv, on Sunday morning. Part of the building collapsed; three bodies have been recovered and rescuers are still digging for 14 people unaccounted for.

Baltic Sea · Shipping

Burning Russian freighter drifts into Finnish waters

The cargo ship Sparta II is still on fire in the Gulf of Finland after a drone strike on Saturday. Finland says Ukraine was the likely attacker; the crew was evacuated and the vessel sits at anchor.

Indonesia · Disaster

Candy factory fire: 3 dead, 36 missing

A blaze swept the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia confectionery plant in Cikande, Serang, on Saturday evening. Three workers are confirmed dead and 36 are unaccounted for as the search resumes on Monday.

Portugal · Protest

Thousands march in Lisbon rain over cost of living

Crowds filled the streets of the Portuguese capital on Saturday, braving heavy rain to protest soaring prices, a housing crisis and wages that no longer stretch to the end of the month.

South Korea · Politics

Choi Kyo-jin resigned after a national exam-cheating scandal engulfed the ministry, the latest blow to trust in the country’s fiercely competitive school system.
